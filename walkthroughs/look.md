# Look around first

1. Click the **beacon icon** in the Activity Bar (left). That opens the Task Beacon sidebar.
2. Expand **Official**, **Private**, and **Agent**. Sample rows are marked `[Sample]`.
3. Click a row. **Summary** (under the tree) shows details.
4. Open the bottom panel — same strip as **Terminal**. The dashboard there is also Official / Private / Agent, not Jira / Wiki / Cron.

Filters on the tree title bar cycle All → Official → Private → Agent.

You are still on samples until you choose a wiki folder. That is expected.
